Looping calculators

Enter asset yield, borrow rate and final LTV; results are per 1 of equity. Add an amount (equity, borrowed or notional) to see all three in dollars

Leverage = 1 / (1 − final LTV). Borrowed per 1 of equity = leverage − 1. Net yield on equity = asset yield × leverage − borrow rate × (leverage − 1). Health factor = liquidation LTV / final LTV; the drop is how far collateral can fall against the debt before liquidation. Starting from a looped position fills in its yield, blended borrow cost and current LTV. Calculators are saved in this browser only.
